Limits

Rule statements 5

  • ⋮⋮
    Order of sections
    Experience, projects, education, skills. Projects only when one answers the posting.
    Changed 12 Oct by Alex Morgan
  • ⋮⋮
    Bullets start with a result
    Each bullet opens with what changed, then how. A number when the claim has one.
    Changed 12 Oct by Alex Morgan

    Oldest experience Changed since 3 Drafts
    Entries that ended before 2020 get one line without bullets.
    Changed 9 Nov by career-agent · was "before 2018"
  • ⋮⋮
    Skills line
    One line, at most eight skills, the posting's own words first.
    Changed 2 Nov by career-agent
  • ⋮⋮
    Links
    Website in the header only. No links inside bullets.
    Changed 12 Oct by Alex Morgan
