Set up your workspace

Openings is operated by your own AI agent. Connect it once; it then builds your career record with you and prepares your first applications. It takes about an hour, most of it without you.

3. First applications

After the record

When the record is complete, this is how every day works:

  1. Collection finds jobs on the schedule of your searches. Jobs that break a rule you enabled are dismissed at once, and can be restored.
  2. Your agent reviews them against your policy, shortlists the credible ones and writes a CV and a letter for each.
  3. You read what is ready, with the posting beside the documents, send the application yourself and mark it applied.
  4. You record the employer's answer. After a few weeks Insights shows which roles and sources answer.
