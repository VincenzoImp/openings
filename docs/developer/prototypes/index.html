Dashboard prototypes

These are static design prototypes for the redesigned dashboard: plain HTML and CSS with synthetic data, no build step and no network resource. They show the information architecture, the design system and the main states of each screen. Tabs, the theme and the density switch work; the other controls only show their state.

Light theme Dark theme The choice is kept for every screen you open from here.

Work

  • Today The home screen: what is ready to apply to, what needs your decision, what happened since your last visit. today.html
  • Review and apply One ready application: the posting, the CV and the letter side by side, download, mark applied or close. review.html
  • Applications The pipeline by stage as a list or a board, recording employer responses, and Insights with response rates. applications.html
  • Jobs Triage: the agent's queue with ingest signals, the shortlist, dismissed jobs with their reason and Restore. jobs.html
  • Activity Questions from the agent, runs with their reports, and one timeline of every change with its actor. activity.html

Library

  • Career The Profile and the career record: entries, skills and claims with typed fields, and a key and value editor instead of JSON. career.html
  • Policy The owner's policy as four lists of statements: decision, eligibility, writing and facts, with rules that use ingest signals. policy.html
  • Templates CV and letter templates: source file, typed limits and rule statements, assets and a preview. templates.html

System

  • Collection Sources and schedules, yield per source, runs and tasks, and provider request limits. collection.html
  • Settings Agent access with scoped tokens, readable skills with local notes, backup and restore, and the workspace. settings.html
  • First run A new workspace: connect the agent, watch it build the record, and learn what happens next. onboarding.html

Shared files: prototype.css (tokens and components), prototype.js (navigation, theme, density, tabs) and screenshots.mjs (captures and checks). Every name, company and address is invented.
